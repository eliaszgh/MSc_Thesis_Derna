GEE was used to prepare key spatial datasets for hydrological analysis in the Wadi Derna catchment, Libya. A 30 m DEM from JAXA ALOS AW3D30 is clipped and corrected to support flood modelling. Land cover is derived from the 10 m ESA WorldCover 2021 dataset. Soil texture information is obtained from the OpenLandMap USDA classification.
